Our Legacy

Founded in 1980, Express Builders Ltd. has grown from a small construction firm into one of Maharashtra's most trusted real estate and infrastructure companies. With 46+ years of experience, we have delivered over 25 projects ranging from luxury residential complexes to large-scale commercial and infrastructure developments.

Our reputation is built on transparency, engineering excellence, and unwavering commitment to deadlines. We are proud to be a preferred partner for government infrastructure projects and private developers alike.
